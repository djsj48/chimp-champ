HOW TO PLAY

GOALPush every other monkey off the stump. The last one standing wins.
MOVELeft thumb: drag anywhere on the left side to steer. Keyboard: WASD or arrow keys.
PUSHTap PUSH (Space) to auto-aim at the nearest rival, or hold and drag to aim yourself, then release. Each attack has a short wind-up, so time it. Rivals caught in the swing get shoved; every hit charges your SUPER. TATA is different: HOLD PUSH (or Space) to keep pecking; her push grows +1 every 0.3 s. The longer you hold, the faster she pecks, but after 3.5 s she needs 2.5 s to recover.
SUPERWhen the yellow ring is full, tap SUPER (M). Momo, Rio, Bruno and Tata can be aimed: hold SUPER, drag to aim, release.
RISING WAVESAfter 30 seconds, foaming waves start rolling in from the edges of the whole map toward the middle. When a wave stops, it vanishes, but everything it passed stays underwater, then it comes again every 15 seconds until only one monkey is left. Fall into the lake or touch flooded ground and you sink: 1 second in the flood and you're out. Check the minimap!
ZOO ROBOTSZoo-bots (200 HP) guard the map. They blast a gas puff from afar to shove you, or fire a net that freezes you for 1.4 s. Watch the warning beam and dodge! Destroy one with pushes: +1 push power for the match and your SUPER fills instantly.
SUPPLY CRATESEvery 40-50 s a 100 HP crate drops into the middle oval. Break it with pushes and a boost banana pops out; run over it to grab it. Gigantic: bigger and reddish, +10 push for 7 s. Bullet: 4x attack speed for 7 s (hold PUSH to rapid-fire). Reflection: for 7 s every attack that hits you (pushes, supers, thrown bananas and acorns, zoo-bot gas and nets) is sent back at whoever launched it. Every boost banana also gives +25% movement speed while it lasts.
TIPSStay near the center, watch your push cooldown ring, and save your SUPER for the moment rivals bunch up. Bushes hide you from rivals until they get close or you attack. Coconuts: break one with pushes (120 total damage) to gain +0.5 push power for the rest of the match. Every rival monkey plays its own style (shown above its name).
